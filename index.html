Frequently Asked Questions

What to know about Mind2Skin.

Is Mind2Skin available in the App Store or Google Play?

Not yet. Mind2Skin is currently being tested through private preview builds for iPhone and Android.

Does Mind2Skin create the final tattoo design?

No. Mind2Skin helps organize and communicate the concept. The tattoo artist remains responsible for the final artwork, suitability, and execution.

Can I use Mind2Skin without creating an account?

Guest exploration is available for parts of the experience, but an account is required to save requests, match with artists, and track appointments.

Does the current preview process real payments?

No. Payment functionality shown in the preview is not yet active and does not process real transactions.

How can an artist participate?

Artists interested in preview testing or future participation can contact us at info@mind2skin.app.
